# Data Inspection Report (SPEC §3.2)

Generated by [`scripts/inspect_data.py`](scripts/inspect_data.py) (`python scripts/inspect_data.py` or `make inspect`). Full console output and plots are written to `outputs/inspection/`. This report records the findings and the preprocessing decisions that follow from them.

## 1. File inventory

- Originally 30 images and 25 labels, matching SPEC §2. After excluding `278`'s label (see §3), the active dataset is **30 images in `data/images/`, 24 labels in `data/labels/`**.
- No orphan labels (every label has a matching image).
- The 6 images without a label are the configured test set: `278`, `535`, `537`, `539`, `551`, `553`.

## 2. Dimensions

All 30 images are 256×256 `RGBA`; all 24 labels are 256×256 grayscale (`L`). No shape or mode mismatches.

## 3. Duplicate / inconsistent labels — `278`'s label is wrong

`270` and `278`'s label files were **byte-identical**, but their satellite images are visually completely different (different roof colors, layout, and surroundings — confirmed in `outputs/inspection/alignment_grid.png`/`outputs/inspection/test_set_preview.png` and by RGB channel means). On inspection it is unambiguous that `278`'s label does **not** correspond to `278`'s image at all — it is `270`'s label, almost certainly copy-pasted onto `278` during annotation. This is a clear labeling mistake, not a legitimate duplicate scene.

**Decision:** training on a wrong label would actively teach the model an incorrect image→mask mapping and degrade prediction quality, so `278`'s label (`data/labels/278.png`) has been **deleted** rather than kept. Its image is not discarded, though — `278` has been added to `TEST_IDS` in `roof_seg/config.py`, so it is treated as a sixth test image and still gets a prediction once the model is trained (no ground truth is needed for the test set anyway). This drops the effective **training** set from 25 to **24** labeled pairs, and grows the **test** set from 5 to **6** images. See the file-inventory check and the duplicate-label note in `scripts/inspect_data.py`, which now expect 30 images / 24 labels / `TEST_IDS = (278, 535, 537, 539, 551, 553)`.

## 4. Alpha channel audit

24 of the 30 images (80%) contain non-opaque pixels (`alpha < 255`); opacity ranges from **73.9%** (`532`) to **100%** (6 images: `121`, `272`, `274`, `284`, `314`, `417`). All 6 test images (including `278`, 98.7% opaque) have some non-opaque pixels (opacity 84.5%–98.7%).

Visual inspection (`outputs/inspection/alignment_grid.png`, `outputs/inspection/test_set_preview.png`) shows this is **not** antialiasing at tile borders — it's rectangular **black censorship boxes** overlaid on the imagery (redacting neighboring buildings/addresses) plus a small "Google / imagery attribution" watermark strip in the bottom-left corner. The masked pixels are RGB ≈ (5, 5, 4), i.e. already rendered black; the alpha channel is low there too but doesn't reveal anything the RGB doesn't already show.

Critically, these masked regions almost never coincide with roof labels: across the 24 active training pairs, only 2 images have *any* overlap between `alpha<255` regions and `label>0` roof pixels, and both are small (`315`: 1.2%, `320`: 0.6% of that image's roof pixels). No test image's roof-relevant area is meaningfully affected either (censorship boxes sit over unrelated neighboring lots, not the target houses).

**Decision:** drop the alpha channel and train on RGB only. The masked regions are already near-black in RGB, so the model sees them as "dark unremarkable area" whether or not alpha is used — no separate handling (e.g. an ignore-mask) is needed given the negligible roof overlap.

## 5. Label value distribution & binarization rule

Aggregated over the 24 active labels: 1,321,236 background px (`0`), 187,452 roof-interior px (`255`), 64,176 boundary px (`1`–`254`, **4.08%** of all pixels). The histogram (`outputs/inspection/label_value_histogram.png`) is strongly bimodal at 0 and 255, with a thin, roughly uniform spread of boundary values in between (antialiasing on polygon edges) — no secondary cluster that would suggest a different label convention.

Comparing binarization rules per image:

| Threshold | Mean roof area | Range |
|---|---|---|
| `label > 0` | 16.00% | 6.09% – 31.48% |
| `label >= 128` | 14.00% | 5.32% – 27.71% |

`> 0` includes on average 1.99 percentage points more roof area than `>= 128` (max difference 3.76pp on image `270`), because it counts all boundary pixels as roof instead of only the darker half of them.

**Decision:** use `mask = (label > 0)`, per the SPEC's canonical rule. It preserves the full annotated roof footprint (including boundary pixels drawn by the labeler as part of the roof polygon) rather than arbitrarily discarding the lighter half of the boundary, and boundary pixels are a small enough fraction (4.08%) that the choice has limited impact either way.

## 6. Class balance

Roof foreground ratio (`label > 0`) per training image ranges from **6.09%** (`274`) to **31.48%** (`270`, now the sole highest outlier after `278` was dropped), mean 16.00%, std 4.99%. Background dominates every image, confirming the class imbalance noted in the SPEC.

**Decision:** use a loss that handles imbalance — BCE + Dice (SPEC default) — rather than plain BCE, and do not rely on pixel accuracy as an evaluation metric.

## 7. Image/label alignment

`outputs/inspection/alignment_grid.png` shows image | label | overlay for 6 randomly sampled training pairs (`241`, `303`, `317`, `324`, `379`, `417`). Roof polygons align tightly with the visible roof outlines in every sample — no offset, flip, or scale mismatch.

## 8. Test-set inspection

All 6 test images (`278`, `535`, `537`, `539`, `551`, `553`) are 256×256 RGBA, same format as training images. `535` and `537` each have a black censorship box; none of the 6 show anything structurally different from the training images (no missing roofs, no unusual color casts). `278` itself looks like an ordinary, unremarkable satellite tile — nothing about the image explains why its label was wrong; the mistake was purely in annotation. `outputs/inspection/test_set_preview.png` has thumbnails with opacity annotated.

## Summary of decisions carried into §3.3

| Decision | Choice | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| Model input channels | RGB (drop alpha) | Alpha only flags already-black censorship boxes; negligible overlap with roof pixels |
| Label binarization | `mask = (label > 0)` | SPEC default; preserves full roof footprint; boundary is only 4.08% of pixels |
| Wrong label (`278`) | **Label deleted, image moved to `TEST_IDS`** | `278`'s label was `270`'s (copy/paste error); training on it would teach a wrong mapping. Kept the image so it still gets a prediction |
| Loss function | BCE + Dice | Roof coverage 6–31% of frame — background-dominated, imbalance-sensitive |
| Test-set membership | `TEST_IDS` in `roof_seg/config.py` (now 6 IDs) | Confirmed to match the 6 images with no label file |
